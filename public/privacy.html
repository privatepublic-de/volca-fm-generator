Privacy

volca fm generator · last updated 25 October 2026

In short: the generator runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking, and it loads nothing from other sites. Your voices and files never leave your computer.

The only personal data involved is your IP address, which GitHub, the host of this page, logs when you open it.

What the generator does in your browser

  • Sound: the browser’s sound is made in your browser, by its own audio engine. Nothing is streamed from anywhere.
  • MIDI: with your permission, the generator sends voices and notes to your volca through your browser’s Web MIDI. It only sends; it reads nothing from your MIDI devices. MIDI data goes only between your browser and the volca.
  • Files: .syx files you open are read in your browser, and the files you export are made there. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Browser storage: these things are kept in your browser’s local storage. volca:app holds the wheel’s pins, the controls and the voice you were working on, so they are there when you come back. volca:favourites holds the voices you keep. volca:player holds the sound settings: the volca model, the MIDI out and channel you chose, where notes play, the volume and the effects. volca:keyboard-folded remembers whether the keyboard is folded away. All of it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes it.
  • Its own files: the generator loads its code and fonts from this same site, nowhere else.
